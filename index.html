Love Letter

for you

my dear,

I know these past few days have not been kind to you. Your period has come again, like a tide that never asks permission, and it has taken so much from you — your ease, your energy, the softness of ordinary hours. If I could, I would carry the ache in your place; and if I cannot, then let me sit beside it, quietly, until the sea remembers how to be calm.

But I could not let these days pass without telling you how thankful I am. Thankful to know you, and more thankful still to have you — someone like you, who is rare the way a sunrise is rare: it returns each morning, and still we forget to be amazed.

Somewhere between your laughter and your silences, I learned that gentleness can be a place to live. You are the quiet kind of light — not the kind that blinds, but the kind that makes everything else in the room more visible, more tender, more true. Because of you, my ordinary days have begun to wear a warmer color.

rest now, my dear.
let the world be small and slow today.
let the blanket be a country,
the tea a quiet hymn,
and let me be the one who stays —
not to mend the weather,
only to sit with you inside it.

I hope you are happy — not the loud, performing kind, but the kind that settles in the chest like warm light in a window: unhurried, unashamed, there to stay. I hope you are loved, in every language a heart can be loved in, and I hope I grow fluent in at least a few of them.

“May you be held as gently as you hold everyone else.”

Most of all, I hope that when you are near me, you feel it — that slow exhale, the shoulders lowering, the sense of a door closing softly against the cold. If I can be one small corner of the world where you feel safe and glad, then I will know I have spent my days well.

So here is my whole wish, written plainly, because the heart is plain: that you are happy, that you are loved, and that you feel happy around me — today, on the heavy days, and on every ordinary day in between.

thank you for being you.
thank you, always.

With love,me
